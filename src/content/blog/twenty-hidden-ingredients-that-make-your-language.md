Quite a few steps ahead of young learners actually. Let's first look at an abreviated list of what the developing mind acquires with their native language. 
1. Isochrony - The arch difference between Germanic and Romance Languages. Romance languages are **Syllable-timed** with each syllable getting roughly the same duration while Germanic languages are **Stress-timed** where only *stressed* syllables take up the same ammount of time. Super difficult to learn.
2. Intonation Contours - The rising and falling intonation of phrases and sentences that can emphasize words, signify sarcasm, questioning or other states. Often responsible for certain accents sounding angry or bored.
