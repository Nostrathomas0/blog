#Are you Sure you're _that_ kind of learner? 

Maybe you took a personality test once and it determined that your kind of personality favors vocal input. Or perhaps your high school teacher was a visual instructor and that really influenced you. Perhaps you say you're a kinesthetic learner because you enjoy video games and you like responsive input. These learning styles have been hugely influential in high schools because they're genuinely inspiring methods for lesson planning, but how can we turn those methods inward for self-directed learning?
