## When and why you should make your own flashcards

Starting a new language can feel like a honeymoon. Your responsibilities are fun and engaging, new concepts are revealing beautiful ideas and the road seems paved with gold. This is the perfect time to set a new habit - making your own flashcards.  If you're a 21st century digital boy or girl, you can use an app but I just go to the stationary store for a block of paper. ![alt text](/images/blog/filename.jpg) These inexpensive, stacks of square paper are useful for all sorts of projects. The pieces are small enough to fit in your pocket and when you're finished into them, you can fold them into origami or dispose of them however you choose.
