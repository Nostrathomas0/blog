## Why recognizing a word isn't the same as using it

Some of the best ways to learn vocabulary are flashcards and apps that present a word on its own — before showing you its definition, forcing you to retrieve meaning rather than just recognize it. Keeping a highlighter and a pen next to you while you read is another simple, nearly free method worth building into a habit. Some apps pair vocabulary with images or audio, which helps anchor the word to something other than a translation.

Each of these methods costs almost nothing, but represents a genuinely crucial part of language learning: the shift from passive recognition to active, retrievable vocabulary.
